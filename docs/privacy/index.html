HERITG Privacy Policy

Effective August 15, 2026

HERITG is developed by Hamanto Studio. It is designed to preserve family trees without an account or sending family information to Hamanto Studio.

No sign-up is required. Family data is stored locally. Core functionality works offline. HERITG does not currently use Firebase, product analytics, advertising SDKs, Sentry, or third-party crash reporting.

Family data

Names, relationships, dates, photographs, notes, and imported genealogy records are stored in the app's local container. Hamanto Studio does not currently operate a server that receives this data.

Imports and exports

Imports are processed on-device. Users explicitly choose where exports are shared. After an export leaves HERITG, the selected service and recipient control it.

Analytics and diagnostics

HERITG does not currently send product analytics or third-party crash reports. Firebase Analytics and Google Analytics are not used.

Platform stores

Apple and Google process downloads, payments, and related platform information under their own privacy policies. Hamanto Studio may receive aggregate App Store and Google Play reports.

Advertising and tracking

HERITG does not sell family data and does not use advertising SDKs, device fingerprinting, or cross-app tracking.

Contact

Email robi@hamanto.com or contact Hamanto Studio through Telegram. Do not include family data, private keys, or credentials in the initial message.
