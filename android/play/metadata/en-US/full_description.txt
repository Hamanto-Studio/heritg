HERITG is a private, local-first home for your family history.

Build and explore your family tree without creating an account. Your family records stay on your device by default, with no ads, analytics, or tracking.

FEATURES

- Create and manage family trees offline
- Record names, dates, places, notes, and profile photos
- Connect parents, children, siblings, partners, and extended family
- Explore your family through an interactive visual tree
- Import and export GEDCOM family-history files
- Create encrypted HERITG backup archives with optional password protection
- Export family charts as PNG or SVG
- Use the app in English or Indonesian

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

HERITG does not require an account and does not automatically upload your family records. You decide when to export or share a file through another app.

PRESERVE YOUR FAMILY STORY

Keep important relationships, life dates, and memories organized in one private archive that works without an internet connection.
