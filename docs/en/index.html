FAQs

Does HERITG upload my family tree?

Not by default. Signing in alone does not upload your family tree. If you activate Family+ and explicitly enable synchronization, HERITG uploads a client-encrypted snapshot and stores server-managed encrypted key recovery data. Your local copy remains available.

Can I use the app without the internet?

Yes. After the web app has loaded once, core editing, imports, and exports work offline. Sign-in, share links, payments, and synchronization need an internet connection. Keep a backup: browser storage belongs to that browser and device.

Is every export encrypted?

Every current .heritg backup uses the encrypted envelope, with an optional password. GEDCOM, PNG, PDF, and SVG exports remain readable files.

How can I verify the privacy claims?

Review the public source code, privacy policy, data processing register, analytics policy, and security process linked on this page.

What does Family+ add?

Your local editing tools, views, and exports remain free. Family+ adds encrypted synchronization between your Web devices and longer share-link durations. It is not a live multiplayer editor. Pay once for six months, one year, or three years; access does not renew automatically.

What platforms are supported?

Use Heritg Web on a phone, tablet, or computer, or download Heritg for iPhone and iPad from the App Store below. The Google Play listing is not available yet. The latest views, Malay language support, and Family+ described here are Web features; native app features may differ.
