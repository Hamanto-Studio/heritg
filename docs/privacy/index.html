HERITG Privacy Policy

Effective September 12, 2026

HERITG is developed by Hamanto Studio. It is designed to preserve family trees without an account or sending family information to Hamanto Studio.

No sign-up is required. Family data is stored locally. Core functionality works offline. Optional account, encrypted sharing and Family+ services use a backend. Optional Web usage statistics are off by default and require consent. No advertising or third-party analytics SDK is used.

Family data

Names, relationships, dates, photographs, notes, and imported genealogy records are stored in the app's local container. Optional sharing and synchronization upload encrypted copies, not plaintext family records.

Imports and exports

Imports are processed on-device. Users explicitly choose where exports are shared. After an export leaves HERITG, the selected service and recipient control it.

Analytics and diagnostics

Where collection is enabled, Web users can choose optional usage statistics in Settings and turn them off without losing features. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control keep collection off. Consent expires after 180 days. Fixed task milestones and outcomes use random, short-lived attempt IDs, never family content, account identifiers, URLs, passwords or screen recordings.

Records expire from reports after 30 days in private Jakarta storage; cleanup and recovery copies may take longer. Unfinished checkout and discovery attempts may bridge a same-tab payment return for up to 24 hours. These statistics are not end-to-end encrypted against Heritg. Authorized operators can separately summarize existing verified billing states without joining browser identities. See the full event, retention and rollout policy. No third-party crash reporting, Firebase Analytics or Google Analytics is used.

Web hosting and payments

Cloudflare hosts the web app and proxies API traffic to Google Cloud in Jakarta. These providers process request metadata and encrypted payloads to operate the service. API responses are not cached; application logs exclude private content and credentials. DOKU processes optional one-time Family+ payments. HERITG stores plan, amount, invoice reference, verified status and access duration, never bank login credentials or family content in payment records. No automatic charge is scheduled.

App Store

Apple processes downloads, payments, and related platform information under Apple's privacy policy. Hamanto Studio may receive aggregate App Store reports.

Advertising and tracking

HERITG does not sell family data and does not use advertising SDKs, device fingerprinting, or cross-app tracking.

Contact

Contact Hamanto Studio through Telegram. Do not include family data, private keys, or credentials in the initial message.
