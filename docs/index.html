Questions, answered

Good to
know.

Is my family data private?

Yes. The core app is local-first. Your data stays on your device unless you explicitly export or share it.

Can I use HERITG without an account?

Yes. The core family-tree experience does not require an account or an internet connection.

Can I bring data from another app?

HERITG supports GEDCOM import and export, as well as its own complete .heritg backup archive.

What platforms are supported?

The native iOS app is available in active development today. Android support and more archive tools are planned.
