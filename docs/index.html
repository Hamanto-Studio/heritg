Protection when it leaves your phone

Back it up.
Lock it first.

Choose password protection for a .heritg backup. Protected archives use authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption with a key derived using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256.

Password protection is optional and applies to HERITG archives. GEDCOM, PNG, and SVG exports are not encrypted by this option.

Good to know

Clear answers.
No fine print.

Does HERITG upload my family tree?

No. The current app stores family data in its local app container. Hamanto Studio does not operate an account system or server that receives it.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. Creating, editing, viewing, importing, and exporting your core family archive does not require a network connection.

Is all exported data encrypted?

No. Password protection is available for .heritg backups. GEDCOM, PNG, and SVG exports are not encrypted, so share them carefully.

Can I verify the privacy claims?

Yes. HERITG is open source. Its privacy policy, data processing register, analytics rules, CI checks, and security policy are all public.

What is available today?

The native iOS app is in active development. Android is planned. HERITG does not offer a web app or hosted family database.
